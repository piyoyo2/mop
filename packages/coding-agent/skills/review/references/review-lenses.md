# Review lenses

Four lenses. Each one checks something the others cannot.

A review runs the lenses that apply and says which it skipped and why. A lens with nothing to report says nothing.

---

## Prose

**Checks:** the quality of the writing itself — genericness, AI tells, cliché, padding, whether the prose is doing work.

**Finds:** a location and what is wrong there.

**Skips:** structure, macros, tokens. Those are other lenses.

---

## Lore

**Checks:** consistency across boxes and across files. Trait clashes, backstory and timeline mismatches, appearance conflicts, a lorebook entry that contradicts a permanent box, a cascade that never reaches an orphan entry.

**Finds:** the two things that disagree, and where each one is.

**Skips:** whether the prose is good. A card can be consistently dull.

---

## Macros

**Checks:** macro correctness. Spaced forms, unknown macros, the "the user" collapse.

**Finds:** the macro, the box, and what it will render as.

**Skips:** macro density as a style question — that belongs to prose.

**Note:** a card with no macros needs no macro lens. Say that you skipped it.

---

## Structure

**Checks:** the shape. Required boxes present, the Scenario frozen into a scene rather than a situation, the Opening Message handing the user a moment, token bands.

**Finds:** the box, the rule it breaks, and the band it lands in.

**Skips:** the writing's quality.

---

## Reporting

- One heading per lens that ran, under that lens's name.
- A finding names a location and a problem. NEVER report a verdict without one.
- A lens that found nothing says nothing.
- Merge findings that two lenses both raised. One problem, one finding.
- A review NEVER rewrites. It reports.
