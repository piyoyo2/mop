# Budget bands

Sizes are token estimates. Report the band, not just the number.

## Permanent fields (Scenario + Personality combined)

| Band | Tokens | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `under_specified` | under 2 000 | Under-written. The character is not specified enough to write consistently. |
| `sweet_spot` | 2 000 – 3 000 | The band to aim for. |
| `lorepacked_range` | 3 000 – 6 000 | Acceptable when the excess is genuinely needed every turn. |
| `approaching_bloat` | 6 000 – 7 000 | Offload now. |
| `bloated` | 7 000+ | Offload before showing this card to anyone. |

## Lorebook hub entry

| Band | Tokens | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `within_aim` | ≤ 300 | Aim here. |
| `above_aim_below_ceiling` | under 1 000 | Over the aim, under the hard ceiling. Split it. |
| `over_ceiling` | 1 000+ | Split it into a hub plus conditional entries. |

## Lorebook conditional entry

| Band | Tokens | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `within_soft` | ≤ 300 | Aim here. |
| `above_soft_within_hard` | ≤ 400 | Over the soft target, under the limit. |
| `over_hard` | over 400 | Offload further or cut. |

## What counts toward the permanent budget

Scenario and Personality only. Example Dialogue is not permanent. Opening Messages are not permanent — but only the selected message loads, so a card with ten slots still ships ten.

## The rule

A band above the sweet spot is a prompt to **offload, not cut**. The content usually belongs in a lorebook entry behind a keyword. Cutting is a last resort and always has to be reported: what was removed and where it went.

## Caveats

- Token counts are estimates from an approximate tokenizer. NEVER present a count as exact.
- The bands are for the whole permanent pair. A single field over 3 000 tokens on its own is a smell even when the total lands in range.
